# Product brief — KI-Training für kleine Betriebe

_Minimal brief from the short intake, 2026-10-28. No market research behind it;
the answers are the vendor's own._

Language: German (Deutsch) — every line the vendor reads is German.

## The idea

An AI training app for retail, trades (Handwerk) and small businesses. It is
sold by Feldmann Consult (Pfungstadt, feldmannconsult.com), who already advises
exactly these businesses on introducing AI.

## The five questions

1. **Who pays, and what do they walk away with?** The **owners** of small
   retail, trade and small businesses pay. They walk away knowing the AI use
   cases for their business — and able to put them into practice themselves
   (vendor's words: "Ich weiß jetzt Anwendungsfälle und kann sie selbst
   umsetzen").
2. **What do they do in it in their first week?** The first lesson, and one
   small task solved in their own business — the app offers a few suggestions
   and guides them through the process.

**Course shape (asked 2026-10-28):** everything open at once, worked through at
their own pace — the self-study course (`docs/courses.md`, shape 1).
3. **How does it bill?** A **one-time payment**.
4. **What is it called?** _open_ — the vendor is unsure. Until decided, the app
   keeps the template's name; it comes back before the salespage.
5. **Logo, house colours, website?** Website: feldmannconsult.com. Found there:
   headings in **Syne**, body text in **Open Sans**, light text on dark picture
   backgrounds. No separate logo file found on the page — _open_ whether one
   exists.

## What the customer gets to SEE (build-app 1b, 2026-10-28)

- **Yes:** a cover picture per lesson, **generated by AI** (~$0.05 each);
  a progress bar ("so weit bist du").
- **No:** lesson videos; a downloadable workbook (PDF). Declined by the vendor
  on 2026-10-28 — goes into `docs/app.md` → *Decisions worth remembering* at
  the first stage.

## What the app DOES alongside the customer (build-app 1c, 2026-10-28)

- **Yes (1):** suggests tasks that fit the owner's business and guides them
  through step by step — reads what they say about their business (trade,
  size, where it hurts). ~1 cent per step.
- **Yes (2):** reads what they write about a task ("so habe ich es umgesetzt")
  and answers with feedback and a next step. ~1 cent each.
- **No (3):** a look back over the course so far.
- ⚠️ For `compliance-check`: AI feedback on what a learner hands in may count
  as *monitoring the learning outcome* (FernUSG, `docs/compliance.md` §6.5).
  Not decided here — flagged.

## Context from the vendor's website

- Audience in their words: owners of small businesses in retail, trades and
  small enterprises, with a local focus (Pfungstadt).
- Their hook: most owners say AI could help them, very few act on it.
- The examples they already offer as consulting: an always-reachable AI phone
  assistant with a morning summary, orders dictated into the phone, AI-run
  marketing (social media, answering enquiries).
- Tone: informal "du" in the offer texts, "Sie" in the company texts.
